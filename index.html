Personal Workspace Connector

A personal integration that lets the account owner work with their Google services from an AI desktop client.

What it does

The connector uses the open-source Google Workspace MCP server to access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts, and Apps Script. It can retrieve information and, when instructed by the account owner, perform supported actions such as updating documents or managing calendar events.

How access works

The account owner authorizes access through Google’s permissions screen. The connector runs on the owner’s computer and uses the permissions granted to it. This website is informational and does not provide a sign-in service or access Google account data.

Personal use

This integration is maintained for the project owner’s personal use. It is not offered as a service for other users and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, OpenAI, or the maintainers of the underlying MCP server.
