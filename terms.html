Personal integration

Personal Workspace Connector is maintained for the project owner’s personal use. This website describes the integration; it does not offer a hosted connector or account-access service to the public.

Authorized use

The connector should access only accounts and resources the operator is authorized to use. Google permissions determine which data and actions are available. The operator is responsible for reviewing instructions, recipients, sharing settings, and consequential changes before carrying them out.

Third-party services

The integration depends on Google services, the open-source Google Workspace MCP server, and the operator’s configured desktop client and AI provider. Their applicable terms, policies, and software licenses continue to apply. The privacy policy describes data handling.

Availability and accuracy

This personal integration is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted operation or error-free output. API changes, authorization expiry, network conditions, or model errors may affect its behavior. The operator should verify important results and keep appropriate backups.

Ending access

The account owner may stop using the connector and revoke its Google permissions at any time.
