Personal desktop integration

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes the Personal Workspace Connector, a desktop integration maintained for the account owner’s personal use, and its informational website.

Google data accessed

The connector accesses the account’s email address and basic profile to identify the authorized account. Depending on the granted permissions and requested workflow, it can access Gmail messages, drafts, labels and settings; Drive files and metadata; calendars and events; documents, spreadsheets and presentations; forms and responses; task lists and tasks; contacts; and Apps Script projects, deployments, execution history and metrics. Granted permissions may also allow supported create, edit, send, share, and delete actions.

Purpose and use

Google data is used to carry out workflows requested by the account owner through their configured client. Access is limited to the permissions granted through Google authorization. The integration is not used to sell Google user data, serve advertising, or build user profiles for advertising.

Storage and transfer

OAuth client credentials and account authorization tokens are stored in private files on the owner’s computer. Google API requests are sent to Google. The connector returns workflow results to the configured desktop client; those results may include Google account content and may be sent to the AI model provider configured in that client. The client and model provider may retain conversations or logs according to their settings and policies. Local client and connector logs may also contain workflow information. Account tokens and private Google content are not intentionally published to this website or its public source repository.

Google API policy

The connector’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data must not be used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The account owner is responsible for configuring any connected AI service consistently with those requirements.

Retention and control

Authorization tokens are kept until removed or revoked. Workflow results may remain in local files, conversations, or provider logs until deleted under the relevant client or provider’s retention controls. The owner can revoke the integration’s permissions from Google Account connections and remove locally stored credentials. Revoking access does not automatically delete data already retained by a client or AI provider; that data must be managed separately.

Website visitors

This website does not include analytics scripts, advertising, account login, or forms that collect Google account content. It is intended to be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and operation of its hosting service; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

The integration is maintained by the owner of the pj-projects-1 GitHub account. The Google consent screen also lists the owner’s support contact.
